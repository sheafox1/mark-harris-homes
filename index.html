Our Story

Our story

Mark Harris has been building homes in Madison County since 1984. A member of the local Home Builders Association since 1986, he served two terms as chapter president, earned the NAHB Certified Green Professional designation in 2006 and was awarded Builder of the Year for the State of Alabama in 2019.
